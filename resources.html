MUN in 60 seconds

Resolution
The formal document a committee writes, debates and votes on. It sets out the problem and the actions to take.
Operative clause
A numbered action in a resolution, starting with a verb such as "Urges" or "Calls upon".
Amendment
A proposed change to a resolution: adding, removing or rewording a clause.
Point of Information
A question to the delegate who has the floor, if they agree to take questions.
Motion
A request to change the flow of debate, such as moving to a vote.
Placard
Your country's name card. Raise it to be recognised by the chair.
